Dharma Tales Junior Publisher

Privacy Policy

What the publisher accesses

The publisher uses the Google Drive API and YouTube API Services. With the channel owner's permission, it reads episode folders, videos, thumbnails, and publishing metadata in the designated Drive queue. It also reads the YouTube channel ID, video IDs, and processing and scheduling status.

How data is used and stored

The publisher processes files on GitHub Actions, sends updated thumbnails back to Drive, and sends videos, thumbnails, and metadata to YouTube for upload and scheduling. It updates completed folder names in Drive. Temporary media copies are discarded after the run. OAuth credentials are stored as secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository. The workflow may retain limited records, such as video IDs and scheduling status, to verify uploads and prevent duplicates. The publisher does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

You can revoke access in Google Account permissions. To request deletion of data retained by the publisher, contact the publisher through the link below. The publisher removes its retained copies within seven calendar days of revocation or a deletion request. You can manage original Drive files and YouTube uploads directly in those services.

See the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the publisher through the project issue tracker. Issues are public. Please leave out personal information and credentials.
